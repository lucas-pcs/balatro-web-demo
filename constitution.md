# Constitution — Balatro Beginner Handguide

Non-negotiable principles. Every other artifact and the final guide must obey these.

## Principles

1. **Beginner-first.** Assume zero prior Balatro runs. Define every term (blind, ante, mult, chips, joker, voucher, consumable) on first use. No jargon without an inline gloss.

2. **Actionable over encyclopedic.** Favor rules of thumb, decision triggers, and checklists over exhaustive card databases. If a sentence doesn't change what the player *does*, cut it.

3. **Scope discipline.**
   - **In scope:** consistently getting a *first win* — beating Ante 8's boss blind — on **White or Red stake** using the base **Red Deck**.
   - **Out of scope (do not include):** endless mode, high-score/scaling theorycraft, all decks, all stakes (Green and above), the full joker catalog, speedrun/challenge runs, modded content.

4. **Cite sources.** Any non-obvious number (scores, payouts, interest caps, hand base values, planet levels) must trace to an entry in `resources.md`.

5. **Scannable while playing.** Short sections. Tables and checklists over prose. **Bold** the decision triggers ("if X, do Y"). The reader is glancing at this mid-game.

6. **Honest uncertainty.** If sources disagree on a number, say so briefly and pick the value from the most authoritative source (the wiki for mechanics).

## Definition of done
The guide satisfies its constitution when a brand-new player, reading only `balatro-handguide.md`, can answer for any round: *what hand am I building, what do I buy, and how much money do I hold?*
