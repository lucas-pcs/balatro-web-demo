## Pre-researched content seeds (verify against sources above)
- **Scoring:** Score = **Chips × Mult**; both compound, so raise the *smaller* factor first.
- **Joker engine:** 1 chip joker + 1 *scaling* mult joker (e.g. Green Joker / Supernova / Ride the Bus type) + as many **×mult** jokers as possible. **Order:** +chips and +mult jokers go *left of* ×mult jokers.
- **Synergy > quantity:** two combo jokers beat five random ones; commit to 1–2 hand types.
- **Planet cards:** most underrated lever — e.g. a Flush at level 5 ≈ 95 chips × 12 mult = 1,140 *before* any joker fires.
- **Economy:** interest = **$1 per $5 held** (cap **~$5/round**); enter blinds with **≥$25**; keep ≥1 economy source in early antes; don't fill all joker slots with pure scorers by Ante 4.
- **Deck thinning:** remove unused cards to draw power hands faster.
