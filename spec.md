# Spec — Balatro Beginner Handguide (WHAT & WHY)

> The "what" and "why." No implementation/formatting decisions here — those live in `plan.md`.

## Why
A new player loses early Balatro runs because they don't know which hand to commit to, how scoring compounds, or how to manage money in the shop. This guide exists to turn those three unknowns into reflexes so the player reliably reaches a first win.

## Audience & goal
- **Audience:** absolute beginner, possibly first time opening the game.
- **Goal:** reliably clear **Ante 8's boss blind** on **White/Red stake**, base **Red Deck**.
- **Success looks like:** the player makes each round's hand/shop/money decision without guessing.

## Required topics — the guide MUST answer each
1. **Core loop.** Blinds (small / big / boss), hands & discards per round, the shop, antes, and what "beating a blind" means (reaching the score requirement).
2. **Scoring.** The **Chips × Mult** formula; why it's multiplicative, so raising the *smaller* of the two factors gives the biggest gain.
3. **Poker hands.** Ranked by practical Balatro value; guidance to **commit to 1–2 hand types** rather than spreading thin.
4. **Planet cards.** How leveling a chosen hand works and why it's the most underrated beginner lever.
5. **Jokers — the engine.** The 3-part template: **1 chips joker + 1 scaling-mult joker + as many ×mult jokers as possible.** Plus **joker ordering**: place +chips and +mult jokers *left of* ×mult jokers, because the board scores left-to-right.
6. **Economy.** Interest (**$1 per $5 held, cap ~$5/round**); enter blinds with **≥$25** for full interest; keep **≥1 economy source** in early antes; don't fill all joker slots with pure scorers by Ante 4; when rerolling is worth it.
7. **Deck thinning.** Removing cards you won't play so you cycle into your power hands faster.
8. **Boss blinds.** How to read the debuff icon and plan a hand around it.
9. **Starter archetypes.** 2–3 safe, beginner-friendly builds (e.g. a Flush build, a Pair/High-Card build) with example joker priorities.
10. **Common mistakes.** A short list of beginner errors, each paired with a one-line fix.

## Acceptance criteria
- All 10 topics present in `balatro-handguide.md`.
- Nothing from the constitution's out-of-scope list appears.
- A **one-page cheat-sheet appendix** lets the player answer hand/buy/money for any round without reading the body.
- Non-obvious numbers trace to `resources.md`.
